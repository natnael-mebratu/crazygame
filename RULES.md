# Crazy — Rules

An Ethiopian shedding card game, played online with friends.
**Goal:** be the first to drop all your cards.

---

## 1. Players & decks

| Players | Decks (52 cards each, no Jokers) |
|---|---|
| 2–3 | 1 |
| 4–6 | 2 |
| 7–8 | 3 |

Maximum 8 players.

Suits: ♥ Hearts · ♣ Clubs ("flower") · ♠ Spades ("spear") · ♦ Diamonds

## 2. Setup

- Everyone gets **6 cards**. The **starting player gets 7**.
- No card is flipped — the table starts **empty** and the starting player opens.
- **Opening:** any single card may open, but a special card (2, A♠, 5, 7, 8, J)
  **loses its power** when it opens. E.g. open with a 2 → nobody draws; if the next
  player then drops a 2, that's a fresh penalty of 2 (not 4).
- Opening with a **combo** is not allowed (foul).

## 3. Your turn

Either:

- **Play** a card matching the **active suit** or the **rank** of the top card, or
- **Draw 1** (required if you don't play). Then play if you can, otherwise **pass**.

Passing without drawing is a foul.

### When a penalty is on you
If a 2 / A♠ was played on you, you either **stack** on it (see below) or **take all
the cards**. After taking them it is your **normal turn**: play, or draw 1 → play or pass.

Trying anything else (another card, or passing) is **caught automatically**: your card
goes back to your hand, you take **what you owed + 4**, and then you make your normal move.

## 4. Special cards

| Card | Effect |
|---|---|
| **2** | Next player draws 2. 2s stack (2 → 4 → 6 …). |
| **A♠** | Next player draws 5. Can stack on a **2♠** (total 7). Stacking only goes small → big: **nothing** goes on top of an A♠. Other Aces are plain. |
| **5** | Skip the next player. |
| **7** | Reverse direction. Another 7 reverses it back. |
| **7 + same suit** | **Combo:** drop the 7 together with any cards of its suit. **No reverse**, and any 2 / A♠ in the combo **doesn't penalize**. |
| **8 / J** | **Wild:** play on anything and name the new suit. Right after another 8 / J you **can** still drop one, but it **can't change the suit** — it plays like a normal card (must match) and the suit stays. Can't be used to dodge a penalty. |

## 5. Crazy! (fouls)

Penalty for any foul: **+4 cards**, and the card(s) go back to your hand.

### Caught automatically
- **Playing out of turn.** The card never reaches the table — the others only see
  that you took +4.
  Exception: before the starter has dropped the first card, **nothing** costs cards — not
  playing early, not LAST!, not CRAZY!. You just get a message to wait.
- **Dodging a 2 / A♠ penalty.** Card goes back, you take owed + 4, and it's still your turn.

### Caught by players
The app **doesn't stop you** from making these mistakes. If someone hits
**CRAZY!** before the next move is made, you're caught:

- wrong suit / rank
- an 8 / J changing the suit right after another 8 / J
- a combo that isn't a 7 + same suit, or opening with a combo
- passing without drawing

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Right call | Move is undone, offender takes +4 and loses their turn. |
| Wrong call (move was legal) | The caller takes +4. |
| Nobody calls | The move stands. You got away with it. |

## 6. Last card — LAST!

When someone is down to **1 card**, the **LAST!** button lights up on every phone.

- **They hit LAST! first** → safe.
- **Anyone else hits it first** → they take **+4**.
- Hitting LAST! when nobody it applies to has exactly 1 card (or too early on yourself) → **you** take +4.

## 7. Draw pile runs out

Keep the top played card, shuffle the rest, and they become the new draw pile.

## 8. Winning

First to an **empty hand** wins — the others get **5 seconds** to call Crazy on that
last card before the win counts.
The last card can be anything.
